Scope

This Notice applies to Personal Information processed by us, including through our website at dynalens.ai (the "Site"), when using our products and services, and when interacting with us directly (collectively, the "Services").

Important note about Customer Data (data processed for our customers): DynaLens's Services are typically used by organizations (our "Customers") who connect their own Dynatrace tenant to the Platform. When a Customer uses the Services to process data from their own Dynatrace environment ("Customer Data"), the Customer decides why and how that Customer Data is processed and is responsible for providing any required privacy notices to their own end users. In these situations, DynaLens processes Customer Data on the Customer's behalf and as permitted by our contract with the Customer — acting as a processor, not a controller.

This Privacy Notice applies to Personal Information that DynaLens processes for its own purposes — for example, information about website visitors, trial and demo requests, account administrators, and communications with us.

How to direct requests: if your request relates to Customer Data processed through a Customer's use of the Services, please contact that Customer first. If you're unsure whether your request relates to Customer Data or information DynaLens controls directly, contact us using the details below and we'll point you to the right path.
